### GitLab CI Integration for Custom Hardware

The Xen Project's GitLab CI pipeline now supports the integration of custom hardware test runners. This feature allows users to test their custom hardware on every build, making it easier for hardware vendors and system integrators to ensure compatibility and performance.

### Key Benefits

- **Custom Hardware Testing**: Add your own test runners to validate your hardware with every new build.
- **Approachable for Vendors**: Hardware vendors and system integrators, such as automobile manufacturers, can seamlessly plug into the GitLab CI pipeline.
- **Continuous Integration**: Ensure that your hardware is always tested against the latest changes in the Xen Project codebase.

### How to Get Started

1. Set up your custom hardware test runner.
2. Integrate the runner with the Xen Project's GitLab CI pipeline.
3. Start testing your hardware on every build.

For detailed instructions, please refer to our [GitLab CI documentation](https://docs.gitlab.com/runner/).

### Join the Community

Members of the Xen Project community are already using this feature to test their custom hardware. Join the conversation and share your experiences on our [Matrix channels](/resources/matrix/) or [mailing lists](/resources/mailing-lists/).
